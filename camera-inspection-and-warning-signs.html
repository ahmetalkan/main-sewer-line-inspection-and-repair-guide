Sewer Camera Inspection Checklist and Early Warning Signs

One slow bathroom sink usually points to a local hair clog in that fixture's P-trap. Multiple lowest-level fixtures reacting together point to the main sewer lateral.

Common main-line symptoms

  • Flushing a toilet makes a nearby shower drain or basement floor drain gurgle or bubble.
  • Running the washing machine sends soapy water backing up out of a basement drain.
  • Repeated backups return within weeks or months of routine cable snaking.
  • Soggy green stripes or sunken soil patches appear above the buried pipe path in dry weather.

What a thorough camera scope should record

Ask the technician to identify the pipe material (PVC, cast iron, clay tile, or fiber-conduit Orangeburg), show the on-screen distance counter from the cleanout out to the municipal tap, note any standing water or offset joints, and locate exact defect spots on the lawn with an electronic sonde locator. Homebuyers inspecting houses older than 30–40 years benefit greatly from ordering a scope during the inspection contingency window. Additional guidance appears on Sewer Line Lab.
