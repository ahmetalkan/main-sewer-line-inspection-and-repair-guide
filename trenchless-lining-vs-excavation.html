Trenchless Sewer Lining vs Open-Trench Excavation

Trenchless technologies can preserve mature trees, stone patios, and concrete driveways—but they are not suitable for every defect shown on a camera scope.

Repair methodWorks well whenKey limitation
Cured-in-place pipe (CIPP) liningHost pipe keeps proper slope and circular shape with cracks or root-prone jointsFollows existing grade—cannot remove a deep standing-water belly or full collapse
Pipe burstingPulling a new HDPE pipe through an aging brittle line between two access pitsStill follows original path grade; tight offsets or severe sags need careful review
Open-trench excavationPipe has collapsed, crushed flat, or has a severe negative-slope bellyDisturbs lawn, roots, or paving above the trench, requiring surface restoration
